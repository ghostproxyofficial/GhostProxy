# Use GeForce Now

You can launch GeForce Now straight from Ghost.

## Launch options

- Open `https://play.geforcenow.com` directly.
- Or open GeForce Now from the Games section.

## Setup advice

- Create your NVIDIA account in advance from home.
- If school networks trigger email verification, pre-authenticate before you arrive, or use your phone for verification.
- Cloud gaming eats bandwidth, so use a stable connection.

# Record lectures with Ghost Notes

Ghost Notes records audio through your microphone, transcribes it into text, and can send it to Duck.ai for a summary. You can find it in the sidebar or by opening `ghost://notes`.

## Recording

- Press Start recording and allow microphone access.
- Speak normally. The transcript appears live as you talk.
- Pause and resume any time. Stop ends the session and keeps the audio for playback.
- Your draft saves automatically, so closing the page will not lose your text.

## Transcription

- On Chrome, Edge, Vivaldi, and other Chromium browsers, transcription happens live while you record.
- On Firefox, live transcription is not available. Record first, then use the Transcribe recording button, which runs an offline model on your audio.
- If no words show up after a few seconds of recording, check that the right microphone is selected and that the tab is still open. Closing the Notes tab stops the session.

## Summarizing

- Press Summarize with Duck.ai once you have text.
- Ghost opens Duck.ai in a new tab with your transcript filled in.
- Press Enter in Duck.ai to get study notes back.

# Hide my tab from teachers

Ghost has privacy oriented launch and cloaking tools.

## About blank launch

- Open Ghost in an `about:blank` context to reduce visibility in common school monitoring workflows.

## Tab cloaking

- In Settings, pick a tab cloak (title and icon).
- This can make the tab look like a standard educational site.

## Important note

- Results depend on school infrastructure and policy enforcement. No method works everywhere, so test yours before you rely on it.

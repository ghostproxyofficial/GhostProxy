# Use Quick AI

Quick AI is the button in the top right. It opens Duck.ai in a small panel so you can ask questions without leaving your tab.

## Basics

- Click Quick AI to open or close the panel.
- Type in the Duck.ai box like normal.
- Clicking anywhere outside the panel closes it.

## Sending screenshots

- Share and send captures your screen through the browser picker.
- Send current Ghost tab captures only your active Ghost tab, with no picker.
- Screenshots go straight into the Duck.ai composer.

## Tips

- You can turn the button off in Settings under Quick AI (Top-Right).
- If the panel looks empty, give Duck.ai a few seconds to load, then reopen it.
